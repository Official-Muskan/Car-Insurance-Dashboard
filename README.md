# 🚗 Car Insurance Dashboard

## Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides insights into car insurance policies and claim amounts. It helps stakeholders analyze insurance trends, customer demographics, vehicle-related risks, and claim distributions through interactive visualizations.

## Dashboard Preview
Insurance Car Dashboard displaying:
- Total Policies
- Total Claim Amount
- Average Claim Amount
- Claim Analysis by Car Use
- Claim Analysis by Car Make
- Claim Analysis by Age Group
- Education-wise Claims
- Gender Distribution
- Marital Status Analysis

## Business Insights
- Private vehicles generate significantly higher claim amounts than commercial vehicles.
- Middle-aged customers contribute the highest claim value.
- Ford and Chevrolet have the highest claim amounts among vehicle brands.
- Customers with bachelor's and master's degrees contribute a large share of claims.
